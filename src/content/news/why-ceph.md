## Proven where it counts

When you buy The Atom, the software protecting your content is not something we wrote last year and hope survives contact with your workflow. It is technology that has been hardened by some of the most demanding data environments on Earth.

## The honest catch — and what we do about it

You get the openness of the platform and the confidence of a product.
