## CCKTech and 33Mega Partner to Sell The Atom

In an exciting development for the media industry, CCKTech has announced a strategic partnership with 33Mega to distribute "The Atom"—a highly reliable file and object storage solution tailored for broadcasters, post-production houses, sports organisations, and archives.

The Atom, built on the robust Ceph platform and leveraging standard Dell hardware, offers a seamless and fully supported storage experience. It is designed to meet the demanding needs of media professionals, ensuring that your data is safe, accessible, and organised. This collaboration allows CCKTech to enhance its product offerings, providing clients with a proven storage solution that prioritises effectiveness and ease of use.

The partnership is driven by a shared commitment to delivering high-quality, dependable technology solutions to the media sector. CCKTech’s extensive experience in technology integration, combined with 33Mega’s innovative storage capabilities, positions both companies to support clients in optimising their media workflows.

"We are thrilled to partner with CCKTech to bring The Atom to a wider audience," said a spokesperson from 33Mega. "This collaboration aligns with our mission to provide accessible, reliable storage solutions that empower media professionals to focus on their creativity without the hassle of managing complex storage systems."

As the media landscape continues to evolve, the demand for efficient and scalable storage solutions grows. The Atom stands ready to meet these challenges head-on, providing an intuitive and effective storage option. With this partnership, CCKTech and 33Mega reaffirm their dedication to supporting the media industry's growth and innovation.
