### At 33Mega, we have three clear priorities that guide everything we do.

#### 1. Protect the Data
Data protection is at the heart of our operations. We understand the critical nature of the information our clients handle. With **The Atom**, built on Ceph and standard Dell hardware, we provide robust file and object storage solutions. Your data is not just stored; it's safeguarded against loss and corruption, ensuring that you can focus on what matters most - your work.

#### 2. Enjoy Life
We believe in creating an environment where our clients can enjoy a balanced life. By streamlining data management and providing reliable storage solutions, we free up valuable time for you and your team. Less time worrying about data means more time to innovate, create, and enjoy life outside of work. We’re here to support a workflow that prioritises your well-being.

#### 3. Lower Lifetime Costs
In today's economy, managing expenses is crucial. Our solutions are designed not only to provide top-tier performance but also to reduce your overall lifetime costs. With **The Atom**, you benefit from a cost-effective, scalable storage system that grows with your needs. We focus on efficiency, ensuring that you get maximum value without compromising on quality.

These three priorities reflect our commitment to our clients and the wider media industry. At 33Mega, we’re dedicated to providing a solution that protects your data, enhances your quality of life, and reduces costs. Together, let’s build a sustainable future for your media storage needs.

For more information about our products and services, please visit our website or contact us directly. We’re here to help you thrive.
