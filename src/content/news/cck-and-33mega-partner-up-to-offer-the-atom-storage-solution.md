## Exciting Partnership Announcement

We are pleased to announce a new partnership between **CCK** and **33Mega**, aimed at bringing our innovative storage solution, **The Atom**, to a broader audience in the media industry. This collaboration combines CCK's extensive experience in technology integration with 33Mega's advanced storage capabilities, ensuring that broadcasters, post-production houses, and archives have access to dependable and scalable storage solutions.

**The Atom** is built on the robust Ceph platform and utilises standard Dell hardware, providing seamless file and object storage that meets the demanding needs of modern media workflows. CCK will leverage their established relationships and technical expertise to position The Atom as a preferred choice for organisations looking to optimise their storage infrastructures.

Both companies share a commitment to delivering high-quality, reliable solutions that empower media professionals. With CCK's proven track record in the tech sector and 33Mega's focus on media industry needs, this partnership is set to create significant value for clients seeking efficient storage solutions.

As media consumption grows and the demand for high-quality content increases, the need for reliable storage has never been more critical. The Atom is designed to provide the flexibility and performance necessary to support the evolving landscape of media production and archiving.

For more information about The Atom and how it can enhance your storage capabilities, please visit [33Mega](https://33mega.com) or [CCK](https://ccktech.com). Stay tuned for more updates as we embark on this exciting journey together.

**About CCK:** CCK is a leading technology solutions provider, specialising in delivering tailored IT solutions across various sectors. Their expertise complements our vision at 33Mega, making this partnership a promising match.

Together, we are committed to empowering every human with 33 megatons of good.
